Best Free Tools for Beginners

You don't need 40 subscriptions. You need a small stack that matches your first project.

For research

Google Trends helps compare search interest. Google Search Console helps website owners understand Google search performance after a site is live.

For design

Photopea runs in a browser, and GIMP is free desktop image editing software. Canva offers a free plan with optional paid features.

For websites

GitHub Pages and Cloudflare Pages can host suitable static websites. These aren't necessarily the easiest option for a full online store.

For email

Systeme.io ★ offers an entry-level option for funnels and email. AWeber ★ provides email marketing tools. Check their current plan limits and prices before choosing.

For video

OBS Studio records screens and video. DaVinci Resolve has a free edition for editing.

Keep it simple

Start with one research tool, one creation tool, and one place to publish. Add analytics and email tools once you have something worth sharing.
