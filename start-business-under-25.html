How to Start an Online Business for Under $25

Start by validating an idea, not buying a pile of subscriptions.

1. Pick one offer

Choose something you can create or deliver: a digital checklist, a basic design service, a simple tutorial, or a print-on-demand design. Describe the intended customer in one sentence.

2. Research demand for free

Look at search suggestions, public discussions, and Google Trends. Note the problems people actually mention. Research is not proof of sales, but it helps narrow your idea.

3. Create a simple sample

Use free design or writing tools to make one useful example. Avoid buying a domain, logo package, or advertising before you know whether people want it.

4. Put it somewhere people can see it

A free profile, marketplace listing, or hosted page can work for your first test. Read the platform's fees and age requirements before signing up.

5. Spend only when needed

Example starter budget: $0 for research, $0 for a sample, $0 for a basic page, and up to $25 reserved for a necessary fee or test. You can spend less; there is no guaranteed return.

6. Learn from real feedback

Ask potential customers what they would change and what they would actually pay for. Improve one thing at a time.
