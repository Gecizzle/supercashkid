How to Launch a Print-on-Demand Store

Test a product idea without purchasing boxes of inventory upfront.

1. Choose a narrow audience

Think of a specific hobby, profession, or interest. A design aimed at one clear audience is easier to describe than a product intended for everyone.

2. Make an original design

Create your own artwork and check spelling at full size. Avoid trademarked phrases, sports team logos, copyrighted characters, or artwork you don't have permission to use.

3. Choose a fulfillment provider

Explore Printify or another print-on-demand service. Compare base product costs, shipping charges, production times, and seller requirements.

4. Calculate a realistic price

Example: selling price $25 minus $13 production, $5 shipping, and $2 platform/payment fees leaves $5 before taxes, returns, advertising, or other expenses. Your actual numbers will vary.

5. Publish a clear listing

Show accurate mockups, sizing details, material information, shipping expectations, and a straightforward description. Check marketplace rules before listing.

6. Order a sample when practical

Inspect print quality and packaging before spending money promoting the item.

7. Test and improve

Share the listing with a relevant audience without spamming. Track clicks and feedback, then improve the design or description.
