Privacy Policy

Last updated October 23, 2026

Super Cash Kid (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates SuperCashKid.com. This policy explains how information is handled when you visit our site, request the free Starter Kit, contact us, or purchase a product.

Information we collect

When you request the Starter Kit, we ask for your first name and email address. You may also enter a mobile number. Our signup service sends your name and email address to our email service provider, Brevo. A mobile number is sent to Brevo only if you check the optional promotional-text consent box. The form also sends your email-marketing and text-marketing choices to our signup service; the current implementation does not maintain a separate consent-history log.

When you email us, we receive your email address and the information you include in your message. If you purchase a product, the payment provider processes payment and transaction information, and information needed to fulfill your purchase may be processed by our services.

How we use information

We use information to process Starter Kit requests, provide downloads and purchased products, respond to inquiries, operate and secure the website, and manage subscriptions. Promotional emails are optional: you can request the free Starter Kit without checking the email-marketing box. Promotional texts also require a separate optional choice.

Services involved

Our site is hosted through Render, and our signup backend uses Brevo to manage email contacts and send emails. Stripe processes payments for products offered through the site. Our domain and email-forwarding services may process information needed to route messages sent to our support address. These providers handle information under their own applicable terms and privacy policies.

Cookies, technical information, and outside links

Our hosting and other service providers may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs to deliver and protect the site. Linked third-party sites, including affiliate partners, may use their own cookies and tracking technologies and have separate privacy policies. Please review those policies before using their services.

Your choices and requests

You can opt out of promotional emails through an unsubscribe link when provided or by contacting us. If promotional text messages are offered, follow the opt-out instructions in those messages, such as replying STOP. To ask about access to, correction of, or deletion of your information, email support@supercashkid.com. We will review requests and respond as required by applicable law; some information may need to be retained for legitimate operational or legal reasons.

Security and retention

We use third-party services to operate the site and handle submitted information. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Information may be retained as needed to provide services, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain business records, subject to applicable law and provider settings.

Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy when our website or services change. We will update the date shown above when we do.
